# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MaintainerFlow a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

We act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Give and receive actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes and repair harm.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses and another person's boundaries.
- Put community safety and project sustainability ahead of individual preference.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, insults,
trolling, sustained disruption, impersonation, doxxing or publishing another person's private
information without explicit permission. Do not pressure anyone to disclose identity, employer,
credentials, private repository content or vulnerability details.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, events and other spaces
where someone represents MaintainerFlow. Maintainers may edit/remove content, reject contributions,
issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary
and proportionate. Enforcement decisions should preserve reporter privacy and avoid conflicts of
interest.

Report conduct concerns privately through a repository owner's private contact channel. For a
security vulnerability, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a conduct report. Reports will be
reviewed promptly, fairly and confidentially to the extent possible. Retaliation against a good-faith
reporter is prohibited. An enforcement decision may be appealed to a maintainer not involved in the
original incident when available.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
